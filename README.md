## What Is Robotics Engineering?

To me, robotics engineering combines the best parts of mechanical, electrical, and computer engineering. It covers mechanism design, embedded control, perception, and system integration, and the hardest part is often getting all of those to work together. I like that range: in one week I might be tuning a PID loop, wiring a robot cell, and writing a vision pipeline.
